# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026

EvoPrompt Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game **Moverse** (the "Game"), package name `com.EvoPromptStudio.Moverse`.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Moverse.

By using Moverse, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Moverse does not require users to create a traditional account with a password.

Depending on the features you use, Moverse and the third-party services integrated into the Game may process certain information.

### Optional Player Profile Information

Moverse allows you to optionally enter a **player name, username, or nickname** that may be displayed within the Game.

Providing a player name, username, or nickname is completely optional. You may use the Game without providing this information.

Moverse also allows you to optionally provide or select **gender information** as part of the in-game profile. Providing gender information is completely optional.

These profile details are currently stored locally on your device and are not intentionally transmitted by EvoPrompt Studio to a remote server.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information or information that you do not want to appear in the Game as your player name or nickname.

### Game and Save Data

Moverse stores game-related information locally on your device in order to provide the Game's functionality.

This may include:

* Game progress
* Level progress
* In-game currency and experience
* Game statistics
* Player name, username, or nickname
* Gender information, if provided
* Game settings and preferences
* Language preferences
* Daily reward information
* Other information required to save and restore your game progress

Local save data is stored on your device using the Game's local storage system.

## 2. Unity Authentication and Player Identifier

Moverse uses **Unity Authentication** with anonymous authentication.

When the Game starts, an anonymous player identifier may be generated and associated with the Game's Unity Services environment. This identifier is used to distinguish one game installation/player from another and to support the Game's backend services.

This anonymous identifier is a technical player identifier and does not by itself identify you by your real-world name, email address, telephone number, or other direct identity information.

Moverse does not require you to provide a password or create a traditional account in order to play the Game.

## 3. Advertising

Moverse displays **rewarded advertisements** through **Unity LevelPlay (IronSource)** mediation.

LevelPlay is used as the advertising mediation platform and may deliver advertisements through third-party advertising networks configured for the Game, including:

* **Google AdMob**
* **Unity Ads**

Advertising and mediation services may process information such as:

* Advertising identifiers
* Device or app identifiers
* Device and technical information
* Operating system information
* App information
* IP address and related technical information
* Information relating to advertisements displayed or interacted with

This information may be used for purposes such as:

* Delivering advertisements
* Measuring advertising performance
* Fraud prevention and security
* Advertising personalization where applicable
* Managing and improving advertising services

Advertising partners may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

## 4. Local Notifications

Moverse may use local notifications to remind players about game-related events, rewards, or other relevant game activities.

The Game may store information such as the last active time or notification scheduling information locally on your device in order to determine when notifications should be scheduled.

Moverse does not require your phone number or email address to send these local notifications.

## 5. Analytics

Moverse does not currently use a dedicated third-party analytics service to track player behavior.

This includes services such as Firebase Analytics, Unity Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, GameAnalytics, and Amplitude.

If analytics or similar tracking services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the applicable data collection and processing practices.

## 6. Information We Do Not Currently Collect Through the Game

Moverse currently does not require or intentionally collect:

* Your email address
* Your telephone number
* Your physical address
* Your precise location
* Your contacts
* Your photos or media
* Your microphone recordings
* Your camera data
* Payment card information
* Purchase history through an in-app purchase system
* Your Google account password

Moverse currently does not use Google Play Games Services for active sign-in, achievements, or leaderboards.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Moverse currently uses certain third-party technologies and services to provide its functionality, including:

* Unity Services
* Unity Authentication
* Unity LevelPlay (IronSource)
* Google AdMob
* Unity Ads
* Google Play Services components required by the advertising infrastructure

These third-party providers may process technical information and identifiers as necessary to provide their respective services.

Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## 8. How We Use Information

Information processed by Moverse or its integrated services may be used to:

* Provide and maintain the Game
* Save and restore game progress
* Provide game functionality
* Distinguish game players using technical identifiers
* Display advertisements
* Measure and manage advertising
* Prevent fraud and abuse
* Maintain security and stability
* Schedule local notifications
* Diagnose technical issues
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 9. Data Sharing

EvoPrompt Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with or processed by third-party service providers when this is necessary to provide Game functionality, advertising, authentication, security, or related services.

For example, advertising-related information may be processed by Unity LevelPlay, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and their applicable advertising partners.

Optional player profile information such as the player name, username, nickname, and gender information is currently stored locally on the device and is not intentionally shared by EvoPrompt Studio with third parties.

## 10. Data Retention

Local game and profile data remains on the user's device until it is deleted, overwritten, or otherwise removed.

Technical information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable provider's policies and retention practices.

We retain information under our control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

## 11. Data Deletion and Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion of information under our control.
